Shen Gui Pai — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-10 · Developer: Shuang Zhou

This policy explains what information Shen Gui Pai (“the Game”) collects, how it is used and protected, and how you can manage or delete it. The Game is an online 1-vs-1 card battle game and collects only the minimum information needed to run.

The Game does not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifier (IDFA). It contains no ads and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Use and sharing

This information is used only to provide the Game. We do not sell, rent, or share it with third parties, except where required by law or necessary to protect players and the service. Verification emails are delivered through an email service provider, which processes your email address only to deliver that message. Your username, avatar and rank are visible to the players you are matched against, to your friends, and on the global leaderboard (top 100 each season); other players can find you by username and send you a friend request; private messages are visible only to you and the other player.

3. Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with TLS; the in-match fast path (UDP) is also encrypted and integrity-checked, with keys delivered only over TLS. Passwords and sign-in tokens are stored on the server only as one-way hashes. The server is located in Japan (Tokyo).

4. Your choices: log out and account deletion

  • Log out: tap your own avatar in the lobby to open your profile, then “Log out”. This removes the saved sign-in token from your device and revokes it on the server.
  • Delete account: in your profile, tap “Delete account” and confirm. The server immediately and permanently deletes your account, password hash, avatar, linked email address, sign-in token, friends, block list and private messages, and removes your username from match records and reports. This cannot be undone.
  • For questions about your information or this policy, contact support@sggame.games.

5. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes

We may update this policy as the Game changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date; significant changes will also be announced in the Game.
